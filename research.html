Research

International finance, market stress, and currency hedging.

My work studies how global financial frictions appear in exchange-rate, swap, bond, and equity markets, especially around crisis episodes.

Published Articles

Extended Market Drivers of CIP Deviations in Korea's Cross-Currency Swap Markets

With F.O. Olarewaju. Paper

We explore the impact of the dollar index, oil prices, sovereign credit default swaps, stock market returns, and Libor-OIS on the Korean won cross-currency basis at one- and five-year maturities. The analysis compares periods before the global financial crisis, during the crisis, during the eurozone crisis, and after the crisis.

Working Papers

A New Testable Theory on Deviations from Covered Interest Rate Parity

R&R, Journal of Derivatives. Paper

I propose an approximate closed-form model for deviations from covered interest rate parity. The model links cross-currency basis swap spreads to credit spread differences, output differences due to technology, and inflation differences, then evaluates those predictions empirically.

Financial Market Linkages in Crises: Evidence from Asia-Pacific and US

With G. Aipoh. Draft soon.

We examine linkages among the United States and eight Asia-Pacific stock markets around major crisis periods. The results suggest that US and Asia-Pacific markets become more strongly linked during and after crises.

Deviations from Covered and Uncovered Interest Parity: A Literature Survey and Lessons Learned

With N. Augustine, F. Olanrewaju, and G. Aipoh. Draft soon.

This survey studies the shifting empirical performance of covered and uncovered interest parity across economies and monetary conditions, especially before and after the global financial crisis.

Cross Currency Basis Swap Spread Spillover

With N. Okafor. Draft soon.

We investigate long-run relationships and shock absorption among major cross-currency bases from 2009 to 2020 using VECM and impulse response methods.

Hedging the Hedges: Can Gold Mitigate Basis Risk?

With N. Augustine and F. Olanrewaju. Draft soon.

This paper studies whether gold can hedge marked-to-market risk for dollar investors using cross-currency basis swaps to hedge foreign fixed-income exposure.

Dynamics of Oil Price and Currency Hedging

With A. Adekanmbi and F. Olanrewaju. Draft soon.

We study how the dollar-yuan cross-currency basis responds to oil-price changes under varying exchange-rate, banking, and credit-risk conditions.

Connectedness of G10 Cross-Currency Basis Swaps

With G. Aipoh and F. Olanrewaju. Draft soon.

We use the Diebold-Yilmaz connectedness framework to study which G10 basis markets transmit and receive shocks, with special attention to the euro basis.

Twin Deficits versus Ricardian Hypothesis: Evidence from the World

With F. Olanrewaju and J. Lokossou. Draft soon.

This project evaluates whether the twin deficits hypothesis or Ricardian equivalence receives broader support across a large panel of countries.

The Effects of Financial Capital Flows on Domestic Investment: New Evidence

With O. Thompson. Draft soon.

Using developing and developed country panels, this study examines whether FDI, portfolio investment, and total capital flows increase domestic investment in host economies.

Macroeconomic Determinants of Stock Market Performance in Nigeria

Undergraduate thesis.

This thesis studies macroeconomic determinants of Nigerian stock market performance from 1981 to 2017.
