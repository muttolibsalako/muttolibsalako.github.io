I am a PhD student in Finance at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley.

My research interests have evolved around financial intermediation, international macro-finance, corporate finance, and climate finance. I am especially interested in how credit, currency, and capital markets transmit shocks across firms and borders.

Before moving to Berkeley, I completed an MA in Applied Economics at Western Michigan University and a BSc in Economics with Operations Research at Crescent University Abeokuta.

Please feel free to reach out to me at absalako@berkeley.edu.
